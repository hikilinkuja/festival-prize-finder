About the data

Winners come from the English Wikipedia lists for each prize, read in November 2026, with recent editions the lists had not yet added checked against festival and trade sources: Sundance 2024 to 2026, the Tiger Award 2025 and 2026, the Golden Shell 2026, Busan 2025 and 2026, Sitges 2026.

Synopses, running times, languages, genres and cinematographers come from each film's English Wikipedia article (text under CC BY-SA 4.0; each card links to its article). They were read automatically, so a synopsis can start mid-story or be missing; about 870 films have one.

  • “Prize level” is my own grouping by how much public attention a prize usually gets, not a ranking of quality. Headline: Palme d'Or, Golden Lion, Golden Bear, Sundance U.S. Dramatic. Runner-up: Cannes Grand Prix, Venice Grand Jury Prize, Berlin Silver Bear Grand Jury Prize. Discovery: Un Certain Regard, Caméra d'Or (first films), Sundance World Cinema Dramatic. Discovery also includes Venice Orizzonti, the Cannes Critics' Week Grand Prize and the Berlin Encounters award (2020 to 2024, when the section existed). Smaller spotlight: Golden Leopard (Locarno), Tiger Award (Rotterdam), Crystal Globe (Karlovy Vary), Golden Shell (San Sebastián), Heart of Sarajevo, Busan New Currents Award, Tallinn Black Nights Grand Prix, Sitges best feature (fantastic and genre cinema).
  • IndieLisboa, FIDMarseille and the Cannes Directors' Fortnight are not included: there is no reliable list of their top prizes to build from, and the Fortnight had no single main prize for most of its history.
  • “Type” comes from the Wikipedia description. Films without an article count as fiction, which is true of most festival winners but not all. For Sarajevo, Tallinn and Busan the country shown is sometimes the director's nationality, as the source lists it.
  • Having no English Wikipedia article is a rough sign that a film is little known, not a measure. For actual reach, look at the number of members who logged the film on Letterboxd.
  • Several early years list more than one winner, either ties or the category prizes Cannes and Berlin gave in their first editions; those categories appear next to the prize.
  • Sundance entries before 2015 often have no director listed in the source. One 1995 entry in the Sundance list (The Young Poisoner's Handbook) is left out because I could not confirm it.
  • Links to Letterboxd, IMDb, MUBI and JustWatch Portugal are title searches, so check the year when several films share a title.
  • “Watchlist”, “Seen” and “Not for me” are kept in this browser, or in your Claude account when the page runs inside Claude and allows it. “Copy my list” gives a short code you can paste into “Import a list” on another device or send to someone.
